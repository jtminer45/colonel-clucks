Our farm

A small farm. A careful batch.

Colonel Clucks Poultry is a small farm in Jos, Plateau State, and a division of Longon Integrated Farms. Batch 1 is deliberately small, so every order gets a personal reply and every bird is accounted for.

We would rather raise fewer birds well than more birds badly. Come December you will know where your chicken came from, and who to call if you have a question.
