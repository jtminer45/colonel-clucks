Colonel Clucks Poultry is a small poultry farm in Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria, and a division of Longon Integrated Farms.

What we do

We raise broiler chickens on our farm and sell them whole or cut, direct to families and tables in Jos. Batch 1 is 50 birds, ready between 18 and 23 December 2026.

How we work

Because Batch 1 is small, every order gets a personal reply. We confirm your price and your day before you pay anything, and you pay when you collect or when your bird is delivered in Jos.
