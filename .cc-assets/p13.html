Why order early

Christmas is the busiest time of year for chicken in Nigeria. Newspapers report each December that prices rise and supplies tighten, and this season industry reporting also mentions a shortage of day-old chicks. You can read more at Nairametrics and Poultry World.

For us the reason is simpler. Batch 1 is 50 birds. When they are spoken for, they are gone, and a pre-order lets you pick your box and your day before the rush.

At home

Storing and cooking your chicken

  • Keep it cold. Put raw chicken in the fridge (4°C or colder) as soon as you get home and cook it within 2 days. Freeze it the same day if you cannot.
  • Cook it through. Chicken is safe when the thickest part of the thigh reaches at least 74°C (165°F) and the juices run clear. See Cornell Cooperative Extension.
  • Keep raw and cooked apart. Use a separate board and knife for raw chicken, and wash your hands after touching it.

Roasting times for each size, thawing times and leftovers advice are in our size guide.
