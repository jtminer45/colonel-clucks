The short version

Rules of thumb for chicken portions

  • Chicken is the main dish: plan on about 450 g of whole bird per adult.
  • A party with many other dishes: about 300 g per adult.
  • Hungry guests or you want leftovers: about 600 g per adult.
  • Children under 12: about half an adult portion.
  • Weights are bird weight with bones. That is why they are bigger than the meat on a plate.

Cooking

Roasting times by bird size

A common oven rule is about 20 minutes per 500 g plus 20 minutes at 180°C. Ovens differ, so use these as a starting point and check the temperature with a meat thermometer.

Is it done?

Chicken is safe when the thickest part of the thigh reaches at least 74°C (165°F). With no thermometer, pierce the thigh: the juices should run clear and the meat should not be pink near the bone. Source: Cornell Cooperative Extension.

Avoid these

Common mistakes when ordering chicken

  • Counting guests but not appetite. A party buffet and a sit-down lunch need very different amounts.
  • Forgetting the bones. A 3 kg bird does not give 3 kg of meat. Use the bird-weight portions above.
  • No fridge space. A 3.5 kg Large box needs a big bowl or tray. Clear space before collection day.
  • A pot or oven that is too small. Check that the bird fits before you order the biggest size.
  • Washing raw chicken. Rinsing splashes bacteria around the kitchen. Cooking is what makes chicken safe, so pat it dry instead.
  • Ordering late. Christmas weekends fill up fast. Reserve early and pick your day.

Our method

How we made this guide

We are a small broiler farm in Jos planning how to match our Batch 1 birds to customers' tables. The portion sizes (300 g, 450 g and 600 g of bird weight per adult) are the rules of thumb we use for that planning. They are not an official standard, and your guests may eat more or less.

The temperature, storage and thawing advice follows widely used home food-safety guidance, and we link a source where we quote a number. Roasting times use a common oven rule and will vary with your oven. Where a figure depends on your kitchen, we say so.

FAQ

Questions

How much chicken do I need for 10 people?

For a normal main-dish meal, about 4.5 kg of bird for 10 adults. For a party with many other dishes, about 3 kg. For hungry guests, about 6 kg. See the lookup table above.

Is one chicken enough for 4 people?

A Standard box (about 3 kg) feeds about 6 adults as the main dish, so one is plenty for 4 adults, with leftovers for the next day.

How much cooked meat do I get from a whole chicken?

Noticeably less than the raw weight, because bones, skin and cooking losses all take their share. That is why the portions on this page are given as bird weight with bones.

What size chickens do you sell?

Three boxes, one bird each: Whole Standard (about 3 kg), Whole Large (about 3.5 kg) and Cut (about 3 kg). We weigh every bird on the sale day and confirm the weight.

Should I wash chicken before cooking?

No. Washing spreads bacteria onto your sink and worktops. Pat the chicken dry with paper towel and cook it fully.

Can I freeze a whole chicken?

Yes. Freeze it the day you get it, in a sealed bag, and use it within 3 months for the best quality. Thaw it in the fridge.
