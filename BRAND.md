# Colonel Clucks house style (always use this)

Everything Colonel Clucks Poultry sends or makes (emails, receipts, newsletters, posts, graphics, documents, messages) follows this guide.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Army Green | #4A5D23 | Header and footer bands, headings, borders, table headers |
| Gold | #E8A317 | Mascot background, buttons, highlights (e.g. balance due) |
| Cream | #FFF6E0 | Page background, text on green |
| Charcoal | #232A14 | Body text, outlines |

Never use other brand colours. White (#FFFFFF) is fine for content cards.

## Type
- Headings: a slab or serif face (Alfa Slab One on the web; Georgia in email and PDF).
- Labels and small caps: Barlow Condensed on the web; Arial, uppercase, letter-spaced in email and PDF.
- Body: Barlow on the web; Arial in email and PDF.

## Layout pattern (emails, receipts, documents)
1. Army Green header band: "COLONEL CLUCKS POULTRY" in cream, with "Farm-raised · Jos, Plateau State" in gold small caps beneath.
2. Gold band with the mascot (saluting for welcomes and greetings, cheering for confirmations and good news).
3. Cream or white content with an Army Green heading.
4. Sign-off: "Have a cluck-tastic day!" then "The Colonel, Colonel Clucks Poultry".
5. Army Green footer: "CONNECT WITH US" in gold, then Instagram @colonelclucks (https://www.instagram.com/colonelclucks/), WhatsApp +234 815 607 8475 (https://wa.me/2348156078475), website https://colonelclucks.netlify.app, email cluckscolonel@gmail.com.

## Mascot
- The Colonel: a cream chicken in an Army Green helmet with a gold star.
- email/salute.png: saluting (welcome emails, greetings).
- receipts/happy-chicken.png: cheering with stars (receipts, order confirmations, good news).

## Voice
- Friendly agric start-up growing in public, with the Colonel's army humour: "Reporting for duty", "troops", "Roger that", "cadet", "at ease", "cluck-tastic".
- Short, warm and plain. Light humour, never at the customer's expense.
- Products are WHOLE or CUT birds only (never "live" or "dressed").
- Never invent prices, weights, deaths or vaccine dates. Prices come from the tracker (Summary!B8); otherwise say "price on request".
